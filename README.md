# National Health Analytics Dashboard 🏥

An interactive **National Health Analytics Dashboard** built in **Power BI**, designed to help hospital administrators and healthcare analysts track patient admissions, demographics, treatment costs, and billing trends across multiple hospitals.

---

## 🔍 Overview

This dashboard analyzes patient admission data across **10 major hospitals** (including Houston Methodist, Johns Hopkins, UCLA Medical Center, Cleveland Clinic, and Mayo Clinic), covering **6 medical conditions** — Arthritis, Asthma, Cancer, Diabetes, Hypertension, and Obesity. It provides a 360° view of patient demographics, admission trends, length of stay, and billing patterns to support hospital resource planning and cost management.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- **Power BI Desktop** – Data modeling, DAX measures, and visualization
- **DAX** – Custom calculated measures (Avg Billing Amount, Avg Length of Stay, YoY comparisons, etc.)
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation

## 📊 Dashboard Features

**Page 1 – Patients Demographics**
- Age Group & Gender distribution (highest volume in 35–49 age group)
- Medical condition selector with descriptive overview panel
- Normal/Inconclusive/Abnormal diagnosis breakdown
- Patient Admission by Hospital & Medical Condition matrix

**Page 2 – Key Trends**
- Admitted Patients by Day Type (Weekday vs Weekend split)
- Admitted Patients by LOS Bucket (Short, Moderate, Long, Extended Stay)
- Monthly & Daily Admission Breakdown table
- Admission Type trend line (Elective, Emergency, Urgent)

**Page 3 – Treatment & Cost**
- Total Billing by LOS Bucket and Admission Type
- Total Billing Accumulated by Hospital
- Prescription volumes across medical conditions (Aspirin, Ibuprofen, Lipitor, Paracetamol, Penicillin)

- **Interactive Slicers** – Filter by Year, Month, Medical Condition, and Hospital

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- **Extended Stay (15+ days)** patients make up **53.35%** of all admissions, driving the largest share of billing
- **Weekday admissions (71.32%)** far outnumber weekend admissions (28.68%)
- **Houston Methodist Hospital** handles the highest patient volume (4,005 total across conditions) and the highest total billing among all hospitals
- **Diabetes (2,750)** and **Hypertension (2,793)** are the two most prevalent conditions by patient count
- The **35–49 age group** shows the highest admission counts across both genders
